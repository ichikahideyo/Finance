# Daymark — Daily Finance Tracker

A responsive, single-page money tracker made with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No installation or account is required.

## Getting started
1. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser.
2. Click **Start fresh** to replace the clearly labeled demo data with an empty tracker.
3. Click **Add transaction** to record an expense or income, with a date and category.
4. Explore **Transactions**, **Budgets**, **Insights**, and **Settings**.
5. Export your transactions to CSV regularly under **Transactions** or **Settings**.

## Features
- Add, edit, search, filter, and delete income/expense transactions.
- Overview totals, savings rate, daily cash-flow chart, and category spending chart.
- Monthly budgets for expense categories and additional spending insights.
- USD, KHR, EUR, or GBP amount display (changing currency does **not** convert values).
- Responsive layout for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- No external dependencies or sign-in.

## Privacy and data limitations
Records use your browser's localStorage. They are not synchronized across devices, accounts, browsers, or private/incognito sessions. Clearing browser storage can remove entries. Back up to CSV regularly. This is a **manual tracker**, not a bank-connected app. The balance shown is the net of recorded transactions, not your bank balance.

## Hosting
The website is static and can be published by uploading `index.html` to any static website host. The sample preview screenshots are optional.
